# Chores User Guide

Chores let household members share recurring responsibilities. Chores are
managed separately from calendar events; chores with due dates also appear on
the household calendar.

## Open household chores

1. Sign in to Roommate OS.
2. Open **My Households** and choose a household.
3. On the household dashboard, select the **Chores** card.

The chores page lists the household's chores, assignees, creator, due date,
and recurrence. Select **Back to dashboard** to return.

## Create a chore

On the chores page, fill in **Add a chore**:

- **Chore name** is required.
- **Description** is optional.
- **Due date and time** is optional. Chores without a due date do not appear on
  the calendar.
- **Repeat** can be Does not repeat, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
- Select one or more household members under **Assign to one or more
  roommates**. At least one household member must be selected.

Select **Create Chore** to save. The page shows a success message and the new
chore in the list. Invalid titles, frequencies, or assignments are rejected
with an error message.

## Edit a chore

Household admins and the chore's creator see an **Edit** button for the chore.
Select it to open the full-page edit form. Change the details or assignees and
select **Save Chore**. The update is reflected in the calendar using the saved
due date, frequency, and assignees.

Other household members can see chores but cannot edit chores they did not
create.

## Delete a chore

Household admins and the chore's creator can select **Delete** next to a chore.
Confirm the browser prompt to remove it. Deletion also removes its assignments,
and the chore no longer appears on the chores page or calendar. Other household
members cannot delete chores they did not create.

## Calendar behavior

The calendar displays dated chores only to their assigned members. A non-
recurring chore appears once on its due date. Daily, weekly, and monthly chores
are expanded into occurrences from the saved due date through the calendar
range being viewed. The calendar uses current chore data, so edits and
deletions are reflected without creating separate calendar events.

## Current limitations

- Chores do not have a completion status, completion history, or overdue
  notifications.
- Rotation of assignments is not supported.
- A recurring chore has no configured end date.
- Household members cannot self-complete or edit chores unless they created
  them or are household admins.
